# Dunvale Market: Defect and Warranty Policy (fictional)

Policy ID: POL-DEF. Cite as "Defect 1.2".
**Applies to:** all orders, alongside whichever return policy version applies.

## 1. Manufacturing defects

1.1 A manufacturing defect that appears within **12 months** of delivery is covered even after the return window has closed.
1.2 The defect must be confirmed by warehouse inspection or a technician report. Damage caused by misuse, drops or liquid is not a manufacturing defect.
1.3 The remedy is **repair or replacement**. A refund is offered only if neither is available within 14 days.

## 2. How it is handled

2.1 The seller arranges the repair or replacement. The marketplace tracks it as a warranty case, not a return.
