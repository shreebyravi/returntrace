# ReturnTrace: Market scan

Checked 12 November 2026 from public pages only. Not finding a feature is not proof it doesn't exist.

| Category | Examples | What they do | Overlap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Returns automation | [Loop Returns](https://www.loopreturns.com/), [AfterShip Returns](https://www.aftership.com/returns), ReturnGO, Redo, ReturnLogic | Return portals, eligibility rules, exchanges, routing | Low: ReturnTrace isn't a portal |
| Return risk scoring | [Loop fraud tools](https://help.loopreturns.com/en/articles/2183937) (risk scoring as returns are submitted; Critical Risk Protection holds critical-risk returns), Claimlane | Flag risky returns before money goes out | Medium: they decide which cases are risky; ReturnTrace investigates the disputed ones |
| Post-purchase experience | [Narvar](https://corp.narvar.com/) | Tracking, returns and exchanges for shoppers | Low |
| Marketplace-native | Amazon, noon | Own return, refund and seller-dispute processes | High inside each marketplace; they don't expose unrestricted data to third parties |

**Name check:** "ReturnWise" (the original working name) is already used by a [Shopify returns app](https://apps.shopify.com/return-wise), so this prototype is called ReturnTrace. No product with that name was found.

## Where ReturnTrace could fit

After scoring and before a decision: the cases that end up disputed, where someone has to rebuild the evidence. Most likely as a feature inside a marketplace's returns operations or a returns platform, not a standalone portal.

## What we couldn't check

Loop's and others' internal investigation tooling; marketplace seller-dispute tools; regional players in the UAE and India; pricing.
